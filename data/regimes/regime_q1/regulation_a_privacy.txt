Regulation A — Data Privacy and Retention Act (Effective Q1 2024)

Section 1. Definitions.
For the purposes of this Act, "personal data" means any information relating to an identified or
identifiable natural person. "Data controller" means any entity that determines the purposes and
means of processing personal data. "Retention period" means the duration for which personal data
may lawfully be stored.

Section 2. Data Retention Requirements.
2.1 Data controllers must not retain personal data beyond the stated retention period unless
    expressly authorized by a subsequent regulatory order.
2.2 Default retention periods by category:
    (a) Financial transaction records: 7 years from the date of transaction.
    (b) Employee records: 5 years from end of employment.
    (c) Customer contact information: 3 years from last interaction, unless consent renewed.
    (d) Medical records processed for billing: 10 years from date of service.
2.3 Data controllers must maintain a retention schedule, updated annually, listing each data
    category, its retention period, and the legal basis for retention.

Section 3. Consent Requirements.
3.1 Processing personal data for marketing purposes requires explicit, affirmative consent.
3.2 Consent may not be bundled with acceptance of general terms and conditions.
3.3 Data subjects may withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal takes effect within 30 days.

Section 4. Breach Notification.
4.1 Upon discovery of a data breach affecting more than 500 individuals, the data controller
    must notify the Regulatory Authority within 72 hours.
4.2 Notification must include: scope of breach, categories of data affected, likely consequences,
    and remediation measures taken or planned.
4.3 Individual notification is required where the breach is likely to result in high risk to
    the individuals concerned, to be sent without undue delay.

Section 5. Penalties.
5.1 Failure to notify within 72 hours: fine of up to 2% of annual global turnover or $10 million,
    whichever is greater.
5.2 Unlawful retention beyond the prescribed period: fine up to 1% of annual global turnover.
5.3 Repeated violations within 36 months are subject to enhanced penalties at double the base rate.
