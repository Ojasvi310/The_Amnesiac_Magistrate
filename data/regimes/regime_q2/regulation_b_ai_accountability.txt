Regulation B — AI Systems Accountability and Transparency Act (Effective Q2 2024)

Section 1. Definitions.
"High-risk AI system" means any automated decision-making system that produces outputs used to
make or substantially influence decisions affecting individuals in domains including: credit and
insurance underwriting, employment screening, clinical diagnosis support, and law enforcement
risk scoring. "Deployer" means any entity that places a high-risk AI system into service or
operates it in the course of commercial activity.

Section 2. Pre-Deployment Assessment.
2.1 Deployers must complete a Conformity Assessment before placing a high-risk AI system into
    service, covering: intended purpose, foreseeable misuse scenarios, accuracy and bias metrics
    disaggregated by protected characteristics, and data governance documentation.
2.2 Conformity Assessments must be updated following any material change to the system's model,
    training data, or operational parameters.
2.3 Assessments must be retained for a minimum of 10 years and made available to the Regulatory
    Authority upon request within 14 business days.

Section 3. Transparency Requirements.
3.1 Individuals subject to decisions made with substantial AI assistance must be informed that
    AI was used, and must be offered a meaningful right to request human review.
3.2 The right to human review must be exercised within 30 days of notification. Deployers must
    respond to human review requests within 21 business days.
3.3 Explanations provided under Section 3.1 must be in plain language accessible to a layperson
    and must not reference proprietary model weights or architecture as a reason for opacity.

Section 4. Incident Reporting.
4.1 Deployers must report to the Regulatory Authority any AI-related incident that results in
    material harm to an individual within 48 hours of discovery.
4.2 "Material harm" includes wrongful denial of credit, insurance, or employment due to AI error,
    or any clinical decision support output that contributed to a demonstrably incorrect diagnosis.
4.3 Deployers must maintain a publicly accessible incident register updated quarterly.

Section 5. Penalties.
5.1 Failure to complete a Conformity Assessment before deployment: fine up to 3% of annual
    global turnover or $15 million, whichever is greater.
5.2 Failure to honor a valid human review request: fine up to 1.5% of annual turnover per
    unresolved request, capped at $5 million per enforcement action.
5.3 Knowingly deploying a system whose Conformity Assessment revealed unmitigated high-severity
    bias: fine up to 5% of annual global turnover; criminal referral possible for senior officers.
