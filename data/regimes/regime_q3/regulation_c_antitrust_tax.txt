Regulation C — Sovereign Wealth Tax and Antitrust Directive (Effective Q3 2024)

Section 1. Definitions.
"Offshore Asset" means any capital, real estate, or equity holding stationed outside the primary jurisdiction of the Sovereign Wealth Fund. "Antitrust Threshold" means a market share exceeding 35% in any domestic critical infrastructure sector.

Section 2. Tax Loop Closures and Reporting.
2.1 All Sovereign Wealth Funds must disclose offshore asset acquisitions to the International Revenue Authority.
2.2 The reporting deadline for offshore asset acquisitions is strictly 90 days from the date of final signature. Failure to report within 90 days triggers an automatic 15% punitive tariff on the total asset value.

Section 3. Antitrust Constraints.
If a multinational bank acting as an intermediary facilitates a merger that crosses the Antitrust Threshold, it must submit a Market Competition Waiver before finalizing the transaction.
